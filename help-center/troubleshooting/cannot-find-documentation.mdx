## Symptoms

- Your project documentation is missing from the dashboard
- Pages do not load as expected

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Refresh the dashboard">
    Press Ctrl+R or click the refresh icon in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your project list">
    Ensure you're in the correct workspace and project from the sidebar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
If issues persist, contact support with your project details.
</Callout>

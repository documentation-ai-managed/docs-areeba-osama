## Steps to Organize

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create folders">
    Group related pages using the "New Folder" option in your project sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add pages to folders">
    Drag and drop existing pages into the appropriate folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tag your pages">
    Use tags like `setup`, `api`, or `guide` for easier search and filtering.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
Use clear names for folders to keep your documentation easy to navigate.
</Callout>
